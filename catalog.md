# Pennydata Apify actors catalog

A plain list of the 14 public Apify actors published under the `pennydata` account. Each line links to the actor page and to its API tab. Prices and limits are on the actor pages and may change.

Disclosure: these actors and this page were built and maintained by an AI agent (Claude) working for the account owner, who has a commercial interest in the actors. They are not affiliated with Apple, Shopify or any site they read from.

- **appstore-reviews-rss**: Fetches iOS App Store reviews and ratings by app ID and country via Apple's public RSS feed. [Actor page](https://apify.com/pennydata/appstore-reviews-rss) · [API](https://apify.com/pennydata/appstore-reviews-rss/api)
- **audio-transcriber**: Transcribes podcast RSS feeds or mp3/wav/m4a/mp4 file URLs to text and SRT/VTT subtitles using local Whisper. [Actor page](https://apify.com/pennydata/audio-transcriber) · [API](https://apify.com/pennydata/audio-transcriber/api)
- **bulk-domain-whois-dns-lookup**: Looks up registrar, creation/expiry dates, nameservers and availability for up to 1,000 domains per run via public RDAP. [Actor page](https://apify.com/pennydata/bulk-domain-whois-dns-lookup) · [API](https://apify.com/pennydata/bulk-domain-whois-dns-lookup/api)
- **bulk-email-validator-no-smtp**: Checks email syntax, MX records, disposable and role addresses in bulk without contacting mail servers. [Actor page](https://apify.com/pennydata/bulk-email-validator-no-smtp) · [API](https://apify.com/pennydata/bulk-email-validator-no-smtp/api)
- **phone-number-validator-offline**: Validates phone numbers in bulk with offline libphonenumber, returning E.164 format, country and line type. [Actor page](https://apify.com/pennydata/phone-number-validator-offline) · [API](https://apify.com/pennydata/phone-number-validator-offline/api)
- **rss-atom-feed-reader-bulk**: Reads RSS, Atom and JSON feeds in bulk (feeds are auto-discovered from website URLs) and returns normalized items. [Actor page](https://apify.com/pennydata/rss-atom-feed-reader-bulk) · [API](https://apify.com/pennydata/rss-atom-feed-reader-bulk/api)
- **shopify-store-leads-contacts**: Extracts public business email, phone, social links and tech stack from Shopify (and WooCommerce) store domains. [Actor page](https://apify.com/pennydata/shopify-store-leads-contacts) · [API](https://apify.com/pennydata/shopify-store-leads-contacts/api)
- **shot-pdf**: Converts a list of URLs to full-page PDF files or PNG screenshots, billed per page. [Actor page](https://apify.com/pennydata/shot-pdf) · [API](https://apify.com/pennydata/shot-pdf/api)
- **sitemap-extractor-plus**: Extracts URLs from sitemap.xml files, including nested indexes, gzip sitemaps and robots.txt discovery, with regex/date filters. [Actor page](https://apify.com/pennydata/sitemap-extractor-plus) · [API](https://apify.com/pennydata/sitemap-extractor-plus/api)
- **video-frame-extractor**: Extracts JPG or PNG frames from direct video file URLs at intervals or scene changes, with an optional contact sheet. [Actor page](https://apify.com/pennydata/video-frame-extractor) · [API](https://apify.com/pennydata/video-frame-extractor/api)
- **website-contact-extractor**: Finds the contact page, contact form, public emails, phones and social links for each website in a list. [Actor page](https://apify.com/pennydata/website-contact-extractor) · [API](https://apify.com/pennydata/website-contact-extractor/api)
- **website-image-extractor**: Bulk-downloads PDFs, images and other files from URL lists with retries and ZIP output, and extracts images from pages. [Actor page](https://apify.com/pennydata/website-image-extractor) · [API](https://apify.com/pennydata/website-image-extractor/api)
- **website-tech-stack-detector**: Detects the CMS, ecommerce platform, analytics, CDN and JavaScript frameworks of websites in bulk, with evidence. [Actor page](https://apify.com/pennydata/website-tech-stack-detector) · [API](https://apify.com/pennydata/website-tech-stack-detector/api)
- **lei-company-lookup-bulk**: Searches the official GLEIF Global LEI Index by company name or LEI and returns legal name, status, jurisdiction and addresses. [Actor page](https://apify.com/pennydata/lei-company-lookup-bulk) · [API](https://apify.com/pennydata/lei-company-lookup-bulk/api)
